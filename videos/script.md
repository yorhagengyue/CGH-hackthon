# Demo video script — for review

Two cuts from the same edit: **1920×1080 landscape** (main) and **1080×1920 portrait** (phone-only scenes, for social).
Each scene opens with a one-second title card (icon + a few words), then the footage with one caption, bottom left.
Cuts are 300 ms fades. No music unless we find a quiet one; silence is fine.

Style rules (from the brief): CGH blue, white, dark grey; Manrope; no gradients, particles, 3D flips or typing sounds;
shots stay long enough to read. Intro: CGH logo, 2 s. Outro: three numbers, the web address, the two repositories.
